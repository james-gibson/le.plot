# le.gume-energy

The starter documents a documentation site. This seed documents a house.

`le.gume-energy` is a public record of one home solar system: production, weather, utility usage,
and the events that explain the gaps. It keeps five of the seven sections, publishes two isotopes,
and adds the thing the starter has none of — a data pipeline. A daily export runs under `launchd`;
chart, universe and intervention data are generated by build scripts; a freshness check exists
because a record with a stale tail is a different claim from a record with a current one.

## Its front door

Rung 0. The homeowner who opens this site wants to know whether the array is losing output, and
will not open a terminal to find out. That constraint is why the site has a `pitch` section at all:
it kept the starter's commercial case and rewrote it for someone deciding whether to act.

## What it proves

The seed can carry a record whose values change: the guard is not only for prose. A build that
checks a page against its repository also checks a page against its data, and the freshness check
is the honest version of "updated regularly".

## What it costs

- **A job that must run.** The site is static, but the export is not: a daily `launchd` agent has to
  succeed for the tail to stay true.
- **A privacy budget.** The record is about a real house. It is non-identifying by construction, and
  every addition has to keep it that way — a constraint the starter never had to state.
- **Five scripts' worth of surface** in a repository cloned from one that argues for a small stack. The
  argument is not that the scripts are free; it is that the numbers are worth them.
