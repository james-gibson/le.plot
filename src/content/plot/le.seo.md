# le.seo

The seed that pitches the other ones. It holds no argument of its own: it holds a **register** of
destinations and two questions, and the combination of the answers is the page.

Most marketing for a family like this fails the same way. A page offers everything to everyone, so
it says nothing, and the reader has to do the choosing. `le.seo` makes the choice explicit: one
**voice** (who is reading) and one **goal** (what the visit should achieve) select a short ordered
set, and the address records both.

## A tree is an address

```text
/v/<voice>/<goal>      sixteen of them, each a prerendered shell
```

Two people who open the same address see the same list, in the same order, because the order is a
rule and not a whim: **readiness first, then name.** The least demanding front door comes first, so
a reader who has not run anything does not meet a toolchain first.

If nothing answers both values the tree falls back — to the voice, then to the whole register — and
says so on the page. An empty pitch page is a worse answer than a broad one, and a page that admits
its basis is better than one that pretends the match was exact.

## The dispatch is the case for keeping the repos

`le.seo` is the second seed that only makes sense because the family is a workspace of separate
repositories. It cannot read their content: it holds a register of twelve names, their URLs, one
line each, and the voice and goal each one serves. That is enough to pitch them, and it is the whole
reason privacy improves when management consolidates — the pitch site holds the *claim*, and the
sites keep the material.

## The isotope

It ships `giardiniera` — a jar of mixed pickled vegetables, arranged rather than mashed: an
assortment served in one order. It is built on `silverskin` (the site shape) and `cornichon` (the
recurring check that the links still resolve), which is the family pattern: a new isotope names what
it adds and does not replace what is under it.

Ten scenarios, canon-clean, with the id printed on the page and re-derived by the guard. The page's
own `scripts/tree-check.ts` runs the same ten against the implementation — 22 assertions.

## What it proves

A pitch can be a data structure. Voice, goal, kind and readiness are four fields; a tree is a filter
and a sort; the address is the citation. The marketing claim and the marketing page are the same
artifact, which is the only way either one stays true.

## What it costs

- **Twelve destinations to maintain by hand.** The register is not derived from the plot, and it
  cannot be: the pitch needs a line of copy per destination, and copy is not a field the map holds.
  A dead link is caught by the build; a stale sentence is not.
- **Four voices and four goals is sixteen pages** with sometimes one destination each. That is the
  honest shape of a small collection, and it reads as emptiness until the collection grows.
- **A pitch with a fixed order can be wrong.** Readiness-then-name is an editorial decision, stated
  so it can be argued with, not derived so it can be defended.
